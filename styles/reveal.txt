Recap a film built on one person being badly underestimated.

Past tense throughout, third person, no "I" and no "you". Sentences run about
ten words, one idea each.

Spend the first stretch on how little they are thought of, and be specific about
it: what was said to them, what they were refused, who laughed. Give it real
room. The payoff is only worth as much as this part earns.

Do not foreshadow. No hinting, no "little did they know", nothing that lets the
viewer guess early. Withhold it completely.

Then state what is actually true about them, once, in an ordinary sentence and
with no build-up to it. After that, show
what they actually do and how the people who dismissed them react -- their faces,
their backtracking, what they lose. Stop at the moment the room understands.

Report what anyone says rather than quoting it. No scenery, no atmosphere, no
commentary on the film, and never describe the shape you are following -- the
viewer should feel the turn, not be told that one is happening.
